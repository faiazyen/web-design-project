Atardecer Bachata Festival

Three days of bachata in Valencia. Sea by day, club by night.

25–27 June 2027 · Valencia, Spain

See the programmeTickets

Three days of bachata in Valencia. Workshops on the sand and in a seaside pavilion by day, socials with DJs, live acts and shows in a club by night. Beginners are welcome, and the Dominican roots of the dance are part of the programme.
