Roots: the story of bachata dominicana

Bachata began in the Dominican Republic and has become one of the most danced Latin styles in the world. It grew in the barrios of Santo Domingo, where guitar-led songs about love and daily life were played at small parties.

A short timeline

  1. 1961The first bachata recordings are made in the Dominican Republic. They are commonly dated to this year.
  2. 1960s–1970sBachata grows in rural and working-class neighbourhoods. Many people look down on the music, and it is rarely played on the radio.
  3. 1980s–1990sBachata becomes popular across the country. Guitar styles evolve, and the music reaches a wider audience.
  4. 2000sModern bachata spreads worldwide. Dancers start to dance it in a close hold, and the sensual style emerges in Europe.
  5. 2019UNESCO inscribes bachata dominicana on its list of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

The sound

Requinto guitar
A lead guitar that plays the melody lines and gives bachata its bright, twanging sound.
Segunda guitar
A rhythm guitar that supports the lead guitar.
Bongo
A pair of small drums that mark the steady pulse the dancers follow.
Güira
A metal scraper that gives the rhythm its fine, scratchy texture.
Bass
Plays a simple repeating line under the guitars.
