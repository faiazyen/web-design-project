Which level am I?

Pick the level that fits you today. If you are between two levels, choose the lower one.

Beginner
You know the basic step or are new to bachata. You can dance part of a song with a partner.
Intermediate
You can dance a full song with turns and you feel the music.
Advanced
You dance often, lead or follow with confidence and want to refine technique.
All levels
Open to everyone. Used for socials, shows and relaxed classes.
