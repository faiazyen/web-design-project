Travel

  • By plane

    Fly to Valencia Airport. The metro runs from the airport to the city in about 25 minutes. A taxi to the beachfront takes about 25 minutes.

  • By train

    High-speed trains arrive at the main station in the city centre. From there, the tram or a bus goes to the beachfront.

  • Between venues

    All three venues are within a 15-minute walk of each other along the beachfront. The festival provides a signed walking route.

  • By bike

    Valencia has a long coastal cycle path. Bikes can be parked next to the Arena Stage and Pabellón Brisa.

Getting here lightly

The festival is held on a public beach. We keep the sea and the sand clean and cut waste where we can.

  • Reusable cups at every bar, with a small deposit
  • Free refill points for drinking water at all three venues
  • A recycling station at each venue and a beach clean-up on Sunday morning
  • Signed walking and cycling routes so you do not need a car
  • No single-use plastic bottles on sale

Accessibility

All three venues have step-free entrances, accessible toilets and a quiet space. This website is built to WCAG 2.0 AA.

  • Step-free access to Arena Stage, Pabellón Brisa and Marea Club
  • Accessible toilets at all venues
  • Quiet rooms at the pavilion and the club
  • Free ear protection at the club bar
  • Seating available at all venues
  • Contact us before the festival if you have specific needs

See the access facts for each venue on the Venues page.

Frequently asked questions

Which workshop level should I pick?

Pick beginner if you have danced bachata fewer than ten times. Pick intermediate if you can dance a full song with turns. Pick advanced if you dance often and want to refine technique. The Which level am I? quiz on the Programme page helps you decide.

Do I need a partner?

No. Workshops rotate partners, and every workshop has a role focus of lead, follow or both. Socials are open to everyone.

What does the Full Pass include?

The Full Pass includes all workshops, all socials, all shows and entry to all three venues for the whole festival.

What should I wear?

Wear light clothes for the day. Bring shoes you can dance in. For the sand, many dancers dance barefoot or in socks. Nights are warm, and the club has a cloakroom.

Are the venues step-free?

Yes. All three venues have step-free access. See the Accessibility page for details on each venue, including quiet rooms and accessible toilets.

When do the night socials end?

Friday and Saturday club socials run until 06:00 or earlier. The Sunday farewell night ends around 02:30. Check each set on the Programme page.

How do I report a problem?

Tell any staff member wearing a Atardecer T-shirt, or write to info@atardecer.example. All reports are handled in confidence. See the Code of conduct page.

Can I transfer or cancel my pass?

You can transfer your pass to another person for free until 14 days before the festival. Cancellations are not refunded.

Code of conduct

Atardecer is a place to dance, and everyone has the right to feel safe, respected and free to say no.

  1. Ask before you dance. A no is a complete answer, with or without a reason.
  2. Respect your partner's boundaries on the dance floor, in dips and in close hold.
  3. Leaders and followers are equal partners. Nobody has to dance with anyone.
  4. No unwanted touching, comments or photos.
  5. Look out for each other. Step in kindly if you see something wrong, or tell a staff member.
  6. Respect all levels. Beginners are welcome, and teaching on the floor is for classes only unless asked.
  7. Alcohol is not an excuse for crossing a boundary.

To report a problem, tell any staff member or write to info@atardecer.example.
