Calculated contrast: text 13.6:1 by day and 15.9:1 at night; button text 5.1:1 and 6.8:1. Meaning never depends on colour alone: level and role are written as text.

Type

Fraunces for headings

Source Sans 3 for body text at 18 px, with a 1.5 line height. Both fonts are free and will be saved with the site, so it works offline. This page shows the closest system fonts until they are added.

Hero plan: two AI-generated stills (Day and Night) with the dates and the Tickets button written as real text on top. A short silent loop video is optional and comes last, with a poster image, a visible Pause button, and no loading on slow connections or for reduced motion.
Why separate: the team rule is mid-fidelity grayscale wireframes. This page is the visual design deliverable for the build, so the wireframe stays easy to judge on structure and function.
